Academics

A structured, progressive educational pathway tailored for every stage of your child's growth.

Nursery School

Ages 3 – 5 years

The nursery years are crucial for cognitive and social development. Our early years program introduces basic literacy, numeracy, shape recognition, and vital social skills like sharing and listening.

Co-curricular activities include structured play, creative arts, and foundational music.

Primary School

Ages 5 – 11 years

Our primary section builds on the nursery foundation, introducing a more structured academic framework across Mathematics, English Language, Basic Science, History, Civic Education, and Information Technology.

Pupils participate in sports, debate clubs, and spelling competitions.

Secondary School

Ages 11 – 17 years

The secondary school prepares students for external examinations (WAEC, NECO, BECE) with rigorous subject teaching, laboratory practicals, and leadership mentorship.

Co-curricular engagements include science clubs, volleyball team practice, debate societies, and community service projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our school.

What age groups do you accept? ▼

We accept children from age 3 (Nursery) through age 17 (Secondary). Our Nursery section serves ages 3-5, Primary 5-11, and Secondary 11-17.

What curriculum do you follow? ▼

We follow the Nigerian national curriculum, preparing students for WAEC, NECO, and BECE examinations. Our approach combines academic rigor with moral development.

How do I apply for admission? ▼

To apply, please contact our school office via phone or email, or use the enquiry form on this page. We will provide you with the admission requirements and schedule an assessment.

Do you offer transportation services? ▼

Please contact our school office directly to inquire about transportation options and availability for your location.

What facilities are available on campus? ▼

Our campus features a modern computer laboratory, science laboratories, conducive classrooms, a school field for athletics, and a volleyball court. Visit our Facilities page for more details.
